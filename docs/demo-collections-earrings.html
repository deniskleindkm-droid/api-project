Note for Dennis: You'd asked for "Shop by Style" (Hoop & Huggie) to be removed here a while back, leaving Stone as the only filter -- brought it back at your call, this time with the full researched taxonomy instead of just one category. 4 real structural types: Hoop & Huggie, Drop & Dangle, Stud, and Tassel & Threader -- pulled from Silverbene's own earring categories (Threader, Dangle, and Gemstone are their real ones) plus the standard stud/hoop/huggie/drop/dangle taxonomy used across jewelry retail. All 39 earrings sorted cleanly into these 4 with zero left untagged -- these are literal, physical categories (does it loop through the ear or sit flush against it), not aesthetic judgment calls, so unlike Statement/Geometric on the other three collections there was no ambiguous middle ground to force a fit into.

Audit pass: checked 10 of the 30 Hoop/Drop-tagged earrings against real photos before finalizing. Lower error rate than the other collections (2 of 10, vs. up to 6 of 9 on Bracelets) precisely because these categories are structural, not aesthetic -- a "Statement Ring" can lie about being bold, but a hoop either loops through the ear or it doesn't. Still caught two real ones: a "Graduated...Hoop" that's actually a vertical leverback drop (moved to Drop & Dangle), and a "Bow Knot...Chain" drop that's actually a tassel design (moved to Tassel & Threader).
