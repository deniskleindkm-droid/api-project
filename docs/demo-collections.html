Note for Dennis: Added two new filters — "Shop by Style" and "Shop by Occasion" — on top of "Discover by Stone." All three combine (e.g. Solitaire → Statement → Moissanite), and every tile's count reflects the other two active filters, so a tile never promises products that don't exist.

Style and Occasion were tagged from the real product descriptions and specs already in our database (design, ring_type, stone-setting fields) — not guessed from product names. The 7 style categories are cross-checked against Silverbene's own published ring taxonomy (their "Open Rings" category is their single largest; they also use "Tennis," "Signet," and "Vintage" as real categories) and 2025-26 market data (solitaire is still the most-sold ring style overall; stacking/thin rings are the current dominant trend). Occasion reuses the exact same 5-tag vocabulary already live on Bracelets, so the site has one consistent mood language instead of a new one per collection. Two rings (Green Cactus Flower, Dual Pear Moissanite) have no real style signal, so they're left untagged for Style rather than force-fit — they still show in the default unfiltered view.

Audit pass: after building this, I checked all 29 "Statement"-tagged rings against their real photos, not just the text — "Statement" was the biggest, most subjective bucket and the one most likely to absorb marketing-copy noise (products literally named "Statement Ring" that are actually delicate, thin bands). Found and fixed 6 mistags this way: 704, 622, 1308 were reclassified to Stacking (thin bands, not bold pieces), 1157 dropped its Statement tag (kept Open & Adjustable), 1291 moved to Solitaire, and 1213 was left untagged. The other 6 style categories were spot-checked but not fully audited the same way — worth doing before this goes live for real.

The stone-filter gap from before still applies: today's material data gives each product exactly one label (e.g. "Moissanite" or "Gold," never both), so rings whose only label is a metal won't appear under any Stone tile — they're still reachable via Style/Occasion or the unfiltered view.
