Note for Dennis: Same three-filter pattern as Rings and Necklaces, with a Style taxonomy built for what Bracelets actually is. "Shop by Lifestyle" already existed here from our first pass -- renamed to "Shop by Occasion" for consistency (same tags, same values, just matching the label used everywhere else now). Style is new: 5 categories -- Tennis, Chain & Link, Charm & Station, Beaded, Nature-Inspired -- cross-checked against Silverbene's own published bracelet taxonomy (Tennis, Chain, Bead, Gemstone, and Charm are their real categories) and 2025-26 market data (tennis bracelets and stacking are the two biggest current trends). That's 2 fewer categories than Rings/Necklaces had -- not every collection needs 7, and forcing more here would've meant padding real categories with weak fits.

Audit pass: checked all 9 "Geometric"-tagged bracelets against real photos before finalizing (same proactive check as Necklaces) and found 6 mistags -- worse than either previous collection. Most were actually chain or braided-chain constructions (a double-strand CZ bolo, a three-tone braided band, a thin rainbow chain) wearing "Geometric" because the product name said so, not because the photo showed an architectural shape; one was a charm-station piece with dangling round stones. After moving all 6 to where they actually belonged, there wasn't a real Geometric category left to keep -- so it's gone, folded into Chain & Link, rather than kept as a thin, dishonest bucket for symmetry with the other collections.

One bracelet (Colorful Mini Round CZ) has no clean fit in any of the 5 categories, so it's left untagged for Style rather than force-fit -- it still shows in the default unfiltered view. Discover by Stone unchanged from the original pass.
