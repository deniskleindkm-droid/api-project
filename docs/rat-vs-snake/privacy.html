Rat vs Snake Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 27, 2026 Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Rat vs Snake is operated by MIKISI LLC (“MIKISI,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and shared when you play Rat vs Snake on iOS, Android, or other supported platforms.

01

Information We Collect

Rat vs Snake does not require players to provide their real name, address, phone number, or payment-card information to play.

The game and integrated services may process:

  • Gameplay information including scores, coins, selected snake, speed mode, revives used, run duration, rats caught, app version, platform, leaderboard position, and related gameplay information.
  • Player identifiers generated through Unity Authentication.
  • Leaderboard information including display name, score, ranking, and gameplay metadata.
  • Device and technical information including IP address, device identifiers, operating-system information, app interactions, diagnostics, crash information, and performance information.
  • Advertising information processed through Google AdMob, including advertising/device identifiers, ad interactions, diagnostics, and information used for advertising measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Purchase and entitlement information required to verify Full Experience purchases.

MIKISI LLC does not receive complete credit-card or bank-account information from Apple or Google.

02

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Operate Rat vs Snake.
  • Maintain gameplay and purchase entitlements.
  • Provide global leaderboards.
  • Prevent fraud and abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve reliability and performance.
  • Display advertising to eligible free players.
  • Process and restore purchases.
  • Comply with applicable legal requirements.
03

Advertising

Rat vs Snake may display advertising through Google AdMob.

Depending on a player's country, device settings, consent choices, and applicable law, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise restricted.

Advertising providers may process device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising information, app interactions, diagnostics, and related information for advertising, analytics, measurement, security, and fraud prevention.

MIKISI LLC does not sell personal information for money.

04

Unity Gaming Services

Rat vs Snake uses Unity Gaming Services, including Unity Authentication and Unity Leaderboards.

Unity Authentication may create a unique player identifier.

Unity Leaderboards may process a player identifier, display name, score, ranking, IP address, and gameplay-related metadata necessary to provide global leaderboard functionality.

We do not intentionally include sensitive personal information in leaderboard metadata.

05

In-App Purchases

Rat vs Snake offers a one-time non-consumable purchase called Full Experience.

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play.

Rat vs Snake receives only the information necessary to determine whether a transaction succeeded and whether the Full Experience entitlement should be active.

Restore-purchase functionality may be used to recover previously purchased entitlements.

06

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the game, maintain purchases and leaderboards, satisfy legal obligations, prevent fraud, resolve disputes, and enforce applicable agreements.

Leaderboard information may remain stored while the leaderboard service is active unless deleted according to applicable deletion procedures.

07

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of certain personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

You may also control certain advertising and tracking permissions through your device settings and consent interfaces presented inside the game.

Privacy requests may be sent to: hello@mikisi.co

We may need sufficient information to identify the relevant player account before fulfilling a request.

08

Children's Privacy

Rat vs Snake is a general-audience game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information directly from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information contrary to applicable law, contact: hello@mikisi.co

09

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information associated with Rat vs Snake.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10

Third-Party Services

Rat vs Snake currently uses third-party services including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Authentication
  • Unity Leaderboards
  • Apple App Store / StoreKit
  • Google Play Billing

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

11

International Data Processing

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than the country where a player lives because our service providers operate internationally.

12

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Rat vs Snake changes, new services are added, or legal requirements change.

The Last Updated date at the top of this page will reflect material updates.
