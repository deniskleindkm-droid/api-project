Autonomous commerce platform

A store that runs itself

Mikisi, an online jewelry brand, operated end to end by software agents.

  • Paid orders flow to the supplier automatically with the exact variant the customer chose, verified before sending.
  • A recovery agent finds orders that failed to reach the supplier, retries them, and alerts the owner only when a problem persists.
  • A signal system processes agent events continuously; stock, catalog, tracking and customer-email agents run on their own schedules.
  • An owner-facing assistant (ARIA) turns plain-language requests into logged, rule-checked actions.
Rolling out
International checkout & pricing

Selling worldwide without losing money

A country-aware checkout and a pricing engine built around a fixed profit target.

  • Structured international addresses, per-country delivery options, and both Stripe and PayPal payments, tested against the real payment sandboxes.
  • Duties, import taxes, shipping and payment fees modelled per market, with every assumption labelled sourced, assumed or unverified.
  • Prices are solved from the target profit and checked by automated tests, rather than marked up from cost.
  • Safety by design: feature flags default off, sandbox payments can never reach fulfilment, and unresolved shipping holds an order instead of guessing.

How we build

Principles behind every system.

  • No manual recoveryIf something fails, a scheduled agent finds and fixes it. Nobody looks up an order ID by hand.
  • One source of truthPrices, stock and customer choices are decided in one place; every agent reads from it.
  • Fail closed, never guessIf a price or shipping method cannot be confirmed, the order is held and retried, not sent on a hunch.
  • Evidence before claimsWe report what was measured, what was assumed, and what is still unverified, and keep them apart.
  • Safe by defaultNew behavior ships behind a flag that is off, is tested in sandboxes, and is switched on deliberately.
  • The owner stays in controlAgents act inside limits the owner defines; anything outside waits for approval.
